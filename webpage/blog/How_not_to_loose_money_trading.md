Ok, so you've decided to make a step further and play with perpetual futures? Read on.

## Don't overleverage
You will probably start on 100x leverage, maybe win and then some time later inevitably loose. At least start small :) And then try to calculate leverage looking at how much the price is changing and what is your target TP (take profit). This is better than "ok, I think this is slow enough".
## Have this stop loss in place
I know you won't. At least until first time you blow your whole position in unexpected turn of events.Then - put it, not mechanically 10%, 20%, but slightly below or above places, where yor setup becomes invalidated. If you aimed for long on pulldown, put slightly below the support line. And have it there at the moment you open position, sometimes entire chart can go down for a second, not enough to change token prioce in the long run, but long enough to liquidate you.
## Don't use your whole account
Well, you can try, but it always ends up the same - and in a sad way. Use some position size calculator, or at least use 1/4 of small account and less if larger.
## Watch out for too big a volatility
Track ATR, watch out for markets waking up (USA, China), you don't want big candles in both direction because you will be stopped out constantly and best case scenario you will be even in the end.
## Don't keep watching your positions all day long
Especially don't try to modify setup all the time. I know, it's hard. But when you try, you usually end up moving stop loss to absurd values, losing whole lot and promising yourself "next time will be better". Set things up, if they don't go your way, adapt, iterate, adjust setup and try again.
## You win by knowing when not to enter
Hardes part, perhaps. There are days, moments, when trading is almost automatical. You put SL and TP, ten minutes later you are 12% up, rinse and repeat. And then either situation changes and somehow you are always on the wrong side of the trend, or you think "it goes so well, I need to trade as much as possible" which is also wrong, because **most of the time you shold be spending not trading**. Checking state of the market, chart shapes, fear & greed index, etc. Strike only when stars align and then keep your gains. You don't have to make 10% few times a day, or even 10% daily. Hell, people are looking far and wide to get 10% IN A YEAR. Dial down expectations, dial up execution.
