Most people don’t lose money in crypto because of hacks. They lose it because they don’t recognize patterns.

Scams don’t look like scams. They look like:

- opportunity<br>
- early access<br>
- insider information

If you discovered it easily, you are not early.

**You are the exit.**
## Never give seed phrase or private key to anyone
Like: never. I know seed phrase is probably more known about, but then some telegram bot app asks you for a private key and you're thinkink "it's a legit app, it's probably how thigns work". Well, prepare to have your wallet cleaned when you least expect it.
## Keep away from memecoins
I know, there is small probability of actually getting lucky and hitting 100x, but for every 100x gain you will loose 10x 99 times. Maybe there was a time when it was enough to "find compelling narrative" and "strong community" and it would actually work, but now it's casino. Just look at amount of tokens generated on pump.fun every minute. It's not enough to do usual check for clusters, honey pots, revoked contract. Best case scenario: slow rug pull, worst: goes to zero in seconds.
## Double check contract address
No, triple check. I was sure so many times that I bought the correct token to be scammed in the end. Some wallets (ekhem, phantom) are more susceptible to this kind of scam, because they are very easy to operate, and so buying false token is also easy. Triple check before buying and then one time after.
## Don't answer for private messages on telegram
Nothing good ever comes from this. Waste of time.
## Adjust privacy settings on telegram
People will try to add you to scam groups. Whenever legit token appears, false one with false group will follow. If you don't disable "anyone can add me to the groups" you will see yourself in a bunch of them, each one claiming to be the only "real" one.
## Give up presales
They never work. I mean, there are two ways you can try to make money on it. 1.) wait until claiming time and sell right away. 2.) buy only after everyoe sold after point 1 and count on token bouncing back. Both ways are highly improbable to succeed. You won't be able to sell in time, slippage will be to high, congestion spike, transfer confirmations will take forever, etc. And tokens bouncing back after panic sell... don't think I have seen any.
## Set slippage
If you set it to "auto" you can be hit by exchange fee of 90%. Never use auto setting.
## Don't trust anyone
Even the guy who tells you to not trust anyone.

People will try to scam you in private messages.

"Pump and dump" groups will turn out to pump for owner only and dump on everyone else.

"Alpha groups" will turn out to be just pump and dump in disguise.

This enthusiastic review of new token reshaping meme coin landscape? Guess what: paid for. No one will hear about it after panic sell when presale ends.

Mev bot, arbitrage bot, automated trading bot? Best case scenario: they don't work. Every other: they scam you either for initial wallet amount, or your passphrase, Or both.

"Created trading bot in python with AI help in 2 hours" - people will usually sell access to their "secret" telegram group with bullshit content.

Just think about it: if someone would have strategy giving them consistent 2% - 10%, why would they ever post about it on twitter? Just make millions in a year and retire.

## All of this repeats
pump & dump groups → only owners win<br>
alpha groups → same thing, different name<br>
influencer reviews → paid<br>
bots → useless or scams

If someone had a real edge, they wouldn’t sell it.

They would use it.

## The uncomfortable truth

- people who got in earlier

- people who are selling to you
