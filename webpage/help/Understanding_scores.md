# Understanding Scores

## MetaScore System

Each ticker is evaluated by a **MetaScore** engine that composes multiple independent sub-scores. The engine supports different combination modes (weighted additive, multiplicative, hybrid-structural) depending on the analysis context.

## Core Sub-Scores

### Sideways (v5)

Detects structural equilibrium — tokens moving within a well-defined range. Factors include:

- Channel structure quality
- Oscillation regularity
- Drift control (how centered is price within the range)
- Volatility consistency
- Extrema detection
- Per-timeframe ideal ATR ranges

High sideways scores mean the token is consolidating cleanly — a potential precursor to a directional move.

### Compression

Measures squeeze conditions — the market coiling before a breakout:

- Width / volatility contraction
- Boundary convergence (upper and lower bounds tightening)
- Directional pressure building inside the squeeze

High compression often precedes explosive moves.

### Breakout (Up / Down)

Scored separately for upward and downward breaks:

- Boundary violation (price escaping the previous range)
- Close conviction (does the candle close beyond the boundary?)
- Volume and volatility expansion confirming the move

These raw scores are then adjusted by the **confidence score** (see v2 Intelligence Scores below) before being displayed in the detail view.

### Trend Predictability

Combines **linear regression R²** (how orderly the trend is) with **normalized slope** (how steep). A high score means the token is trending in a clean, predictable direction.

### Gain / Loss

Simple close-to-close return over the evaluation window.

## v2 Intelligence Scores

These appear in the detail view below the chart:

### Setup Quality

Three independent trade setup ratings:

- **Compression breakout** — is a squeeze about to resolve?
- **Trend continuation** — is an existing trend likely to persist?
- **Range reversion** — is price likely to bounce off support/resistance?

Each is scored by an isolated evaluator to prevent metric contamination.

### Confidence Score

A unified 0–1 indicator answering **"how much should I trust this setup right now?"** Combines four factors with regime-specific weights:

| Factor | Trend regime | Sideways | Compression |
|--------|-------------|----------|-------------|
| Trend health | 40% | 10% | 20% |
| Market health | 30% | 30% | 30% |
| Crowding (inverted) | 20% | 30% | 20% |
| Volatility fit | 10% | 30% | 30% |

Displayed as a color-coded dot next to the setup quality score:

- 🟢 **Green** (> 0.75) — High confidence: context strongly supports the setup
- 🟡 **Yellow** (> 0.55) — Medium confidence: some headwinds
- 🔴 **Red** (≤ 0.55) — Low confidence: treat the setup score with caution

The confidence score also gates notifications — setups with confidence below 0.6 are filtered out of setup-of-the-day alerts.

### Confidence-Aware Breakout Probability

Raw breakout-up and breakout-down scores are adjusted by the confidence level before display:

> probability = base score × (0.5 + 0.5 × confidence)

- **Full confidence (1.0)** → raw score unchanged
- **Half confidence (0.5)** → raw score × 0.75
- **Zero confidence (0.0)** → raw score × 0.5, with an additional 20% penalty

When trend health is weak (< 0.4), up-breakout probability is further reduced by 30% to avoid false bullish signals in deteriorating trends.

The breakout bars in the Metrics Breakdown section show a confidence-colored dot alongside the percentage, matching the setup quality dot.

### Fragility

Measures crowd-positioning risk — how vulnerable the token is to a liquidation cascade:

| Component | Weight |
|-----------|--------|
| Funding rate extremeness | 25% |
| Open interest expansion | 30% |
| Long/short imbalance | 20% |
| Liquidation proximity | 25% |

Also exposes the **squeeze direction** (long squeeze vs short squeeze) and **directional side**.

### Retail Behavior

Four behavioral dimensions derived from positioning signals:

- **Greed** — excessive risk-taking
- **Fear** — defensive positioning
- **Patience** — measured accumulation
- **Panic** — rapid unwind

Aggregated across all 150 tokens for a market-wide behavioral snapshot.

### Signal Labels

Up to 3 plain-English labels per token, generated by a deterministic rule engine:

- *"Breakout pressure building"*
- *"Crowded longs"*
- *"Compression squeeze active"*

Each label includes a **strength** value and **type** (info / warning / opportunity).

## Market-Level Intelligence

### Market Regime

The overall market is classified into one of four states:

- **Compression** — volatility contracting, market coiling
- **Sideways** — range-bound, low directional conviction
- **Trend** — sustained directional movement
- **Expansion** — high volatility, wide swings

Confidence scores and breadth breakdowns show how unanimous the signal is across tokens.

### Transition Probabilities

Heuristic model predicting the likelihood of regime shifts. Key inputs: compression breadth, volatility slope, market dispersion, and how long the current regime has lasted.

## Limitations

- Scores are derived from historical candle data and positioning metrics
- They describe **current structure**, not future price action
- Fragility and behavior scores depend on funding/OI data availability
- Always combine with your own analysis before trading
