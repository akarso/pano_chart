# Getting Started

## Install

Download Pano Charts from Google Play and open the app.

## Browse the Grid

The main screen shows up to 150 crypto tokens as a scrollable sparkline grid. Each row displays a mini chart, the current price, and key scores — all color-coded so you can spot opportunities at a glance.

- **Pull down** to refresh data (auto-refresh available with Pro)
- **Scroll** through ranked tickers
- **Tap** any row to open the full detail view
- **Long-press** a ticker to add it to your favourites

Tickers are sorted by a scoring algorithm that adapts to the selected timeframe. You can change sort criteria, toggle columns, filter stablecoins, and switch timeframes from the settings overlay.

## Detail View

Tap any ticker to open the detail screen with:

- **Interactive candlestick chart** — pinch to zoom, drag to pan, tap-and-hold for crosshair with price/time readout
- **Indicators** — RSI, EMA clouds, and ATR, togglable from the settings icon
- **Volatility activity overlay** — color-coded bars showing which hours (or days on the 1D chart) are historically most active
- **Macro events** — economic calendar events plotted directly on the chart, including future events projected ahead on the time axis
- **Score breakdown** — sideways, compression, breakout, and trend scores for the current timeframe
- **Setup quality** — independent ratings for compression breakouts, trend continuations, and range reversions, with a confidence dot (green/yellow/red) showing context reliability
- **Confidence-adjusted breakout bars** — breakout-up and breakout-down probabilities scaled by signal confidence, with matching confidence dots
- **Fragility gauge** — crowd-positioning risk (funding, OI, liquidation proximity)
- **Retail behavior** — greed, fear, patience, and panic dimensions
- **Signal labels** — up to 3 plain-English signals (e.g. "Breakout pressure building")
- **Trade buttons** — one-tap links to open the symbol on your preferred exchange

## Other Screens

- **Bubble Map** — explore a market-cap bubble visualization
- **Fear & Greed** — market sentiment gauge, always one tap away
- **Macro Events** — full calendar view with country and impact filtering
- **News** — curated crypto articles without leaving the app

## Free vs Pro

The free tier includes the sparkline grid, detail charts with indicators, score breakdowns, and the bubble map. **Pro** (€4.99/month, 14-day free trial) unlocks:

- **Auto-refresh** — live updates across all views, from 10-second intervals on 1m charts to hourly on dailies
- **Full token list** — access to the complete token universe
- **Market Pulse** — market regime detection, breadth analysis, composite index, and transition forecasts
- **Social feed** — social and macro event feeds
- **Advanced notifications** — full notification settings
- **Future axis** — extended chart timeframe support
- **Extended macro events** — deeper economic calendar data
- **Retail behavior** — greed, fear, patience, and panic dimensions per token
- **Token diagnostics** — diagnostic tools per symbol
