Privacy Policy for Pano Charts

Effective date: 2026-03-10

Pano Charts ("the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and your rights regarding your data when using the App.

1. Information We Collect

Pano Charts is designed to minimize data collection. We do not collect personal information unless it is necessary to provide core functionality.

The App may collect the following limited information:

Email Address

If you create an account or make a purchase, your email address may be collected. This information is used solely for:

  • Account identification
  • Payment processing
  • Providing access to purchased features
  • Customer support communication

No other personal data is intentionally collected.

2. Automatically Collected Information

The App does not collect analytics data, location data, device identifiers, or usage tracking information.

Standard technical information may be processed by the operating system or app distribution platforms (such as the app store) for installation, updates, or security purposes. This data is handled by those platforms according to their own privacy policies.

3. Payment Processing

Payments made through the App are processed by the official payment systems of the platform on which the App is distributed, such as the payment services provided by the app store. Pano Charts does not store credit card information or other payment details.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

Information may only be disclosed if required by law or necessary to protect the security or integrity of the App.

5. Data Retention

If you create an account, your email address may be stored for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the limited data we store. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be posted within the App or on the distribution page where the App is available.
