App is still fresh and a big update is available already:

## What's Included

- **Market Pulse** — real-time market regime detection (compression, expansion, trend, sideways) with transition probability forecasts and regime history tracking
- **Composite index** — aggregated market-wide price chart for quick macro context
- **Setup quality scores** — independent ratings for compression breakouts, trend continuations, and range reversions
- **Unified confidence score** — multi-factor reliability indicator (trend health, market health, crowding, volatility) shown as green/yellow/red dots
- **Confidence-aware breakout probability** — raw breakout scores adjusted by signal confidence so weak-context spikes don't mislead
- **Position crowding & fragility** — funding rates, OI data, and liquidation proximity gauges reveal when the crowd is dangerously one-sided
- **Retail behavior engine** — greed, fear, patience, and panic dimensions per token to help trade against the herd
- **Signal labels** — up to 3 plain-English labels per token ("Breakout pressure building", "Crowded longs") with strength indicators
- **Volatility activity overlay** — color-coded bars on the chart showing historically active hours/days, derived from 150 days of minute-level data
- **Social feed** — follow accounts, view posts in-app, with notification routing and per-account settings
- **Notification engine** — unified alerts for social posts, news, macro events, market regime changes, and setup triggers with dedup and rate limiting
- **Trend health layer** — detects weakening trends and breakout risk, feeding into setup scoring as a context modifier
- **Enhanced trend detection** — direction agreement penalty, shape validation, cluster gating, and a new "silent" regime for flat-volume markets
- **Auto-refresh** — staggered live updates across all views (Pro), from 10-second intervals on 1m charts to hourly on dailies
- **Pro subscription** — €4.99/month via Google Play with a 14-day free trial, unlocking auto-refresh, full token list, Market Pulse, social feed, and more
