## PR-098b: feat(market_state): Sector rotation card

🎯 Objective

Show sector relative-strength rotation on the Market Pulse screen and let a
tap overlay a sector's sparkline on the composite chart.

See ROADMAP Track C — PR-098. Depends on PR-098a (backend `/api/market/sectors`).

🧱 Scope

- `SectorRotationData` / `SectorIndexData` — parse `GET /api/market/sectors`
  (`id`, `name`, `symbolCount`, `points`, `return`, `rs`, `rsAvailable`,
  `marketSymbolCount`)
- `HttpSectorRotationApi` — 404 (catalog not configured) maps to `null`
  ("feature disabled"), not an error
- `buildSectorRotationRows` / `sectorRsLabel` — normalized bar fraction +
  formatted RS label, kept out of the widget per FRONTEND.md
- "Sector rotation" card on `MarketPulseScreen`: horizontal RS bars, sorted
  RS-available-first descending (backend order)
- Tap a sector row → overlay its own sparkline on the Composite Index chart
  (`CompositeChartPainter.overlayPoints`)

Out of scope: backend sector math (PR-098a).

1️⃣ Data

Parse `sectors[]` with nullable `rs` (omitted/absent ⇒ unavailable). A 404
response means the sector catalog isn't configured; the card is simply
absent, same treatment as any other optional Market Pulse card.

2️⃣ Card

Horizontal bar per sector, width = `|rs| / max(|rs|)` among RS-available
sectors (0 when unavailable). Label shows `+x.x%` / `-x.x%` / `0.0%`, or `—`
when RS is unavailable. Only RS-available sectors are tappable.

3️⃣ Overlay

Tapping a sector plots its own `points` series as a second stroked line on
the composite chart, scaled independently (own min/max) so the shape reads
even when the sector's length or magnitude differs from the composite
series. Tapping the same sector again clears the overlay. The selection is
cleared if the sector disappears from a later response (e.g. it drops below
the min-symbols threshold).

🚀 Result

Market Pulse shows sector RS rotation as a bar chart beneath the composite
index, matching the shared-window RS contract from COMMON.md. Tapping a
sector previews its sparkline on the same chart without navigating away.
